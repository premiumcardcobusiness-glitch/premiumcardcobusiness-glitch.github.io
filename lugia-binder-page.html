The Lugia binder page

Put one Lugia from each era in the nine pockets, oldest top-left and newest bottom-right, and the page reads like a timeline. The Neo Genesis Lugia from 2000 opens the top row, and Lugia ex from Prismatic Evolutions, 2025, closes the bottom one.

One pocket that breaks the pattern

Pocket two is the crystal Lugia from Aquapolis. Its number, 149 of 147, is higher than the set's own count, and its Crystal Type power changes its type for the turn when Fire, Water or Psychic Energy is attached. The 30th Celebration set brought it back in its Classic Collection in 2026, so a reprint can hold that pocket while you look for the original.

Three layouts, compared

1. Oldest to newest. The page pictured above. One Lugia per era, read like a timeline.

2. Lugia and Ho-Oh, facing pages. Lugia is the legend on the cover of Pokémon Silver and Ho-Oh the one on Pokémon Gold, so nine Lugias on the left page and nine Ho-Ohs on the right make a spread that matches the two games.

3. Johto's legends page. In a Pokédex binder with each region on fresh pages, Johto runs from 152 to 251 over 12 pages. Lugia is number 249, so it lands in pocket eight of the eleventh page, between Tyranitar and Ho-Oh, with Raikou, Entei and Suicune on the same page. Celebi, number 251, sits alone on the last page.

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type Lugia. The builder lists Lugia cards with the set each one comes from. Add a word from the set name, such as Lugia Neo Genesis, to see only that set's cards.
  3. Tap one card for each pocket, oldest first. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Lugia cards are there?

Dozens, once promos, jumbo cards and every version count. That is far too many for one page, so a Lugia page is a choice of nine: one per era, one per card type or your nine favourite drawings.

Which Lugia starts the page?

The holo Lugia from Neo Genesis, 2000, the set that brought the Johto Pokémon to the card game.

Which Lugia ends the page?

Lugia ex from Prismatic Evolutions, 2025, card 82 of 131.

Where does Lugia go in a Pokédex binder?

Lugia is number 249. With each region on fresh pages, Johto runs from 152 to 251 over 12 pages, and Lugia lands in pocket eight of the eleventh, between Tyranitar and Ho-Oh, on a page that also holds Raikou, Entei and Suicune.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
