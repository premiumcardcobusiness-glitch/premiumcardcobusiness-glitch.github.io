How many binder pages do I need?

Divide your cards by the pockets on one side of a page and round up: that is your page sides. Halve it and round up for two-sided sheets. On nine-pocket pages a sheet holds 18 cards, so 300 cards take 34 sides, or 17 sheets.

300 cards on 9-pocket pages: 34 page sides, that is 17 two-sided sheets. 6 pockets stay empty for new cards. Binders: 1 of 20 sheets (360 cards each).

The count tells you what to buy. The fun part is what goes on each page, and that is worth trying before a single card goes in a sleeve.

How many cards a binder holds

Pockets per side, times two sides, times the sheets in the binder. A binder with 20 sheets holds 160 cards on 4-pocket pages, 360 on 9-pocket pages, 480 on 12-pocket pages and 640 on 16-pocket pages. Check how many sheets your binder takes before you buy pages for it.

Count first, then plan the look

A set, a Pokédex or a colour theme only looks right when each page does. Plan the pages before you buy sleeves:

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type a card name and tap the printing you want in each pocket.
  3. Tap See page. Swap cards until the page feels right, then plan the next one.
  4. Leave a pocket or two free at the end of a set or a region, so new cards slot in without moving everything.

Questions

How many cards fit on a 9-pocket page?

Nine on each side, so 18 on a sheet when you use both sides.

How many pages for 1,000 cards?

On nine-pocket pages: 112 sides, or 56 two-sided sheets. That is three binders of 20 sheets.

Should I use both sides of a page?

Most collectors do, since it halves the sheets. One side only keeps the backs visible and the pages lighter.

Is the page builder free?
