How to scan a whole binder page in one photo

Scanning cards one at a time takes forever. With Beltstra you photograph the whole 9-pocket page and every pocket is read and priced at once. It's free and runs in your phone's browser.

Try a page now, no account

The four steps

  1. Open Beltstra in your phone's browser, tap Scan, then Page.
  2. Lay the binder flat in even light and take one photo of the whole page.
  3. Check the nine results. Each pocket shows the card's name, set and market price, and the page shows its total. A card the app is not sure of is marked, so you can tap the right printing before you trust the price.
  4. Turn the page and repeat. The cards fill your binder in the app pocket by pocket, in the same order as your real one.

Getting a clean read

  • Fill the frame with the page, straight on rather than at an angle.
  • Glare is the main enemy on holo and full art cards: move the lamp or tilt the binder a little.

What you can do with the scanned page

  • See what the binder is worth, card by card and in total, re-priced every morning.
  • Rearrange it: drag a card to another pocket. Sell or trade one and Undo puts it back where it was.
  • Show it off: share the binder with a link. Friends see the cards and market prices, never what you paid.
  • Take it with you: download the whole collection as a spreadsheet any time.

How long does a binder take?

A 9-pocket page is one photo, so a 360-card binder is about 40 photos. The free plan covers 1,000 card scans a month, which is a couple of full binders.
