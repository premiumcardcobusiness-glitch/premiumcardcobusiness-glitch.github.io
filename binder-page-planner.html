How it works

  1. Open the page builder and pick a character: Luffy, Goku, Charizard, Pikachu, Eevee, Gengar or Mewtwo. Or type any card name.
  2. Tap the cards you want, in the order you want them in the pockets. Nine fill a page.
  3. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it off, or Add cards to try another.
  4. Send page gives you a link that shows a picture of your page. Keep page saves it as a binder in a free account.

Questions

Is the planner free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.

Does it show prices?

The planner shows names and pictures only. To see what a card is worth, look it up on Card prices or scan it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, on iPhone and Android in the browser, and on a computer. There is nothing to download.
