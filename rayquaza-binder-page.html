The Rayquaza binder page

Put one Rayquaza from each era in the nine pockets, oldest top-left and newest bottom-right, and the page reads like a timeline. Rayquaza ex from EX Dragon, 2003, opens the top row, and a gold Mega Rayquaza ex from Delta Reign, 2026, closes the bottom one.

Two pockets that break the pattern

Pocket three is a delta species card from Dragon Frontiers, so this Rayquaza ex is a Lightning type with a gold border instead of the usual look. Pocket nine is the gold Mega Rayquaza ex from Delta Reign, the Mega Evolution set released in November 2026. Put it last and the page ends on its one gold card.

Three layouts, compared

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type Rayquaza. The builder lists Rayquaza cards with the set each one comes from. Add a word from the set name, such as Rayquaza Evolving Skies, to see only that set's cards.
  3. Tap one card for each pocket, oldest first. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Rayquaza cards are there?

Dozens, once promos, jumbo cards and every version count. That is far too many for one page, so a Rayquaza page is a choice of nine: one per era, one per card type or your nine favourite drawings.

Which Rayquaza starts the page?

Rayquaza ex from EX Dragon, 2003, a shiny silver-bordered ex card.

Why is the last Rayquaza gold?

Pocket nine is a gold Mega Rayquaza ex from Delta Reign, the Mega Evolution set released in November 2026. Ending on the one gold card makes the page finish bright.

Where does Rayquaza go in a Pokédex binder?

Rayquaza is number 384. Hoenn runs from 252 to 386, which is exactly 15 pages of nine, so Rayquaza lands in pocket seven, bottom-left, of Hoenn's last page. Every Pokémon on that page is a legendary or a mythical.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
