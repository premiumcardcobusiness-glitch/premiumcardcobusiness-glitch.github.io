Organize your binder

How to organize Pokémon cards in a binder

Sort by set, then by the card number printed near the bottom of each card, and leave an empty pocket for every card you don't have yet. That is the one common order where a new card never pushes the others along: it goes straight into the pocket that was waiting for it. Sorting by Pokédex, type or color works too, as long as you leave spare pockets at the end of each section.

Five ways to sort, compared

Pick one and stick to it. Switching halfway through a binder is what turns sorting into a weekend of moving every card.

One order to skip: by price. Prices change all the time, so the order would too, and you would be reshuffling every month.

Find any card's page and pocket

Most cards print a number such as 4/102 near the bottom: card 4 of a set with 102 cards. It sits in one of the bottom corners. With nine pockets on each page side:

  • Page: divide the card number by nine and round up.
  • Pocket: the remainder. A remainder of zero means pocket 9.

So card 40 always lives on page 5, pocket 4, whether you own cards 1 to 39 yet or not.

On a 12-pocket page, divide by 12 instead. Secret rares are numbered past the set total, such as 172/165, so they just carry on after the last regular card.

Plan the first page before you sleeve

Sorting is easier to judge on a page than in your head. Try the order on one page first and see if you like how it looks before you move a single card.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type a Pokémon's name. The builder lists its cards with the set each one comes from.
  3. Tap cards in the order you want them. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

What is the best way to organize Pokémon cards in a binder?

For completing sets, sort by set and then by the card number printed near the bottom of each card, and leave an empty pocket for every card you do not have yet. New cards then go straight into their waiting pocket and nothing else moves.

Should I leave empty pockets for missing cards?

Yes, if you sort by set and number. Every card then has a fixed page and pocket, the gaps show what is missing at a glance, and a new card never pushes the others along.

Where do secret rares go?

After the last regular card of the set. A secret rare is numbered above the set total, such as 172/165, so it simply continues the count.

Where do Trainer and Energy cards go?

In set order they have their own numbers, so they go in their numbered pocket like any other card. If you sort by type, give them their own section at the back.
