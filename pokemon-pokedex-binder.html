Pokédex binder

How many pages does a Pokédex binder need?

Every Pokémon through Generation 9 is 1,025 cards: 114 nine-pocket page sides, or 57 two-sided sheets. That fits in three 360-card binders with a few pages to spare. Start each generation on a fresh page and it is 117 sides (59 sheets), still three binders if a region can run on into the next one.

Starting each region on a fresh page has a bonus: the first nine Pokémon of almost every region are its three starters and their evolutions, so page one of nearly every region is its starter page. Starter page guide

A 360-card binder holds 40 sides, so three of them take all 117 with three to spare. To keep every region inside one binder, use four: Kanto and Johto (29 sides), Hoenn and Sinnoh (27), Unova, Kalos and Alola (36), then Galar and Paldea (25).

Setting it up

  1. Pick one card per Pokémon to start. Any printing counts. Forms like Alolan Vulpix or Mega Charizard add many more pockets, so most collectors fill every number first and add forms later.
  2. Start each region on a fresh page. Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Kalos, Alola, Galar and Paldea all open with their starters, so page one of each region is three full evolution lines. Unova is the one exception: Victini is #494, one pocket before Snivy. Sinnoh's last page has exactly one empty pocket, if you would rather give Victini a home there.
  3. Leave empty pockets empty. A gap in the right spot shows what you still need far better than a list.
  4. Keep a few spare pages at the back. New Pokémon keep arriving, and Paldea's last page has six free pockets already.
  5. Use side-loading pockets if you can. Cards slide in from the side, so they don't fall out when the binder tips.

Try a page before you move a card

  1. Open the free page builder and type a Pokémon's name. Tap the printing you want.

Questions

How many pages for the original 151?

17 page sides: 16 full pages and 7 cards on the 17th. That is 9 two-sided sheets.

How many binders for every Pokémon?

Three 360-card binders hold all 1,025, even with each generation on fresh pages. Four let every region sit inside one binder.

Is the card number the Pokédex number?

No. The number at the bottom corner is the card's place in its set. Newer cards also print the Pokédex number near the bottom, like NO. 0001 for Bulbasaur.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
