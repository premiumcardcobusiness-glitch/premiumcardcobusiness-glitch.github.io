Eevee family page

The Eeveelution binder page

Eevee has eight evolutions, so Eevee and its family fill a nine-pocket page exactly, with no gaps and nothing left over. Put Eevee in the middle pocket and its evolutions around it, or line them up in Pokédex order. Both look complete, which makes it an easy first themed page.

Three layouts, compared

1. Eevee in the middle. The page pictured above. The three stone evolutions, Vaporeon, Jolteon and Flareon, run along the top. Espeon and Umbreon, the day and night pair, sit either side of Eevee. Leafeon and Glaceon take the bottom corners with Sylveon between them. Every evolution sits right next to Eevee.

2. Pokédex order. Eevee first, then each evolution by its number. It reads left to right like the Pokédex, and it matches the order of a Pokédex binder if you keep one.

3. One card from each era. Mix old and new: Eevee, Vaporeon, Jolteon and Flareon are all in the Jungle set from 1999, and the newer evolutions joined the cards in later years. The page then shows the family's whole history, with a different card style in almost every pocket.

One set, matching borders

Cards from the same set share one frame and one style, so the page looks like it was made to go together. Prismatic Evolutions (2025) has a regular card of Eevee and of each of the eight evolutions, which is what the page at the top of this guide is made from. Any set works for the idea, as long as it has all nine.

Two pages, face to face

Open a binder and two pages sit side by side. Put the regular cards on the left and the special versions, the full arts and ex cards, on the right in the same pocket order. Each evolution then faces its twin. For more pages built around one Pokémon, see the favorite Pokémon binder guide.

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type a name, such as Vaporeon. The builder lists its cards with the set each one comes from.
  3. Tap one card for each Pokémon, in the order you want them. Pockets fill in the order you tap. For the family layout: Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Eevee, Umbreon, Leafeon, Sylveon, Glaceon.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Eeveelutions are there?

Eight: Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon and Sylveon. With Eevee that makes nine, exactly one nine-pocket binder page.

Is there one set with Eevee and all eight evolutions?

Yes. Prismatic Evolutions (2025) has a regular card of Eevee and of each of its eight evolutions, so the whole page can share one set's look. The page pictured in this guide is made from those nine cards.

Which pocket should Eevee go in?

The middle one, pocket 5, so every evolution sits around it. If you sort by Pokédex number instead, Eevee (number 133) goes first, in pocket 1.

Where do the full art and ex versions go?

On the facing page, in the same pocket order. Open the binder and each evolution's regular card faces its special version.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
