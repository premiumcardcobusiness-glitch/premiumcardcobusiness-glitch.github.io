The Mewtwo binder page

Put one Mewtwo from each era in the nine pockets, oldest top-left and newest bottom-right, and the page reads like a timeline. The Base Set Mewtwo from 1999 opens the top row, and Mewtwo ex from the 30th Celebration set, 2026, closes the bottom one.

One pocket that breaks the pattern

Pocket two is Shining Mewtwo from Neo Destiny: Mewtwo in its rare shiny colours instead of the usual purple. Its number, 109 of 105, is higher than the set's own count, so it was one of the set's secret cards, and it is the only shiny Mewtwo on the page.

Three layouts, compared

1. Oldest to newest. The page pictured above. One Mewtwo per era, read like a timeline.

2. Mewtwo and Mew, facing pages. Mewtwo was made from Mew, so nine Mewtwos on the left page and nine Mews on the right make a spread where each pair faces across the binder.

3. The end of Kanto. In a Pokédex binder, Kanto's 151 Pokémon fill 17 pages. Mewtwo is number 150, so it lands in pocket six of the last page, after Zapdos and Moltres and the Dratini line, and right before Mew. The last two pockets stay empty until Johto starts on a fresh page.

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type Mewtwo. The builder lists Mewtwo cards with the set each one comes from. Add a word from the set name, such as Mewtwo Base, to see only that set's cards.
  3. Tap one card for each pocket, oldest first. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Mewtwo cards are there?

Dozens, once promos, jumbo cards and every version count. That is far too many for one page, so a Mewtwo page is a choice of nine: one per era, one per card type or your nine favourite drawings.

Which Mewtwo starts the page?

The holo Mewtwo from Base Set, 1999, card 10 of 102.

Which Mewtwo ends the page?

Mewtwo ex from the 30th Celebration set, 2026, in one of its full-art prints.

Where does Mewtwo go in a Pokédex binder?

Mewtwo is number 150. Kanto's 151 Pokémon fill 17 pages, and Mewtwo lands in pocket six of the last one, after Zapdos, Moltres, Dratini, Dragonair and Dragonite and right before Mew.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
