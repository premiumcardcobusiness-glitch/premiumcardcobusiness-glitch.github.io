The Charizard binder page

Put one Charizard from each era in the nine pockets, oldest top-left and newest bottom-right, and the page reads like a timeline. The Base Set Charizard from 1999 opens the top row, and the Scarlet & Violet 151 Charizard ex from 2023 closes the bottom one.

Three Charizards in other colours

Most of the page is orange, which is what makes three pockets stand out. Pocket two is Dark Charizard, from the Team Rocket set that brought in the first Dark Pokémon. Pocket seven is black, because shiny Charizard is black, and it comes from the Hidden Fates Shiny Vault. Pocket eight is the rainbow Charizard VMAX from Champion's Path. If you want the page to stay mostly orange, keep it to one of the three; if you like them, give them a page of their own.

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type Charizard. The builder lists Charizard cards with the set each one comes from. Add a set name, such as Charizard Base, to see only that set's cards.
  3. Tap one card for each pocket, oldest first. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Charizard cards are there?

Dozens, once promos, jumbo cards and every version count. That is far too many for one page, so a Charizard page is a choice of nine: one per era, one per form or your nine favourite drawings.

Which Charizard starts the page?

The Base Set Charizard, 4/102, from 1999, the first English set. Dark Charizard from Team Rocket and the Expedition Charizard follow it on the top row.

Why is one Charizard black?

Shiny Charizard is black. The Charizard GX in pocket seven comes from the Hidden Fates Shiny Vault, which printed shiny Pokémon.

Can I swap in a different Charizard?

Yes. Type Charizard and a set name in the builder, such as Charizard Base, and tap the card you want. Tap a card on the page to take it out.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
