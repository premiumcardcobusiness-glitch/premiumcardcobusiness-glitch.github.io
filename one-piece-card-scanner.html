Built for how One Piece is printed

  • Parallels and alternate arts are priced apart from the base card. When two printings look alike, the card is marked unsure, so you tap the right one before you trust the price.
  • Manga rares, Super Rares and Leaders are read like any other card: name, set, number and market price.
  • Foils look like foils. In your binder, foil printings shine in the app the way the real card does.

What "free" means here

  • 1,000 card scans every 30 days, up to 300 a day. One scan is one card identified and priced.
  • Your first three weeks are bigger: 2,000 scans, 500 a day, and every Pro tool.
  • No payment details. When the three weeks end you simply stay on Free; nothing is charged.
  • No cap on your collection. Keep as many cards and binders as you like.

One card or a whole page

Add card photographs a single card. Page photographs a 9-pocket binder page and reads every pocket, so a binder of One Piece cards goes nine at a time instead of one. Each card lands in your collection with its set, number and market price, and the page shows its total.

Where the prices come from

TCGplayer market prices, matched to the exact printing where we can and refreshed every morning. They are estimates for your own records, not offers to buy.

Questions

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, both, and on a computer with a camera. Open it in the browser and add it to your home screen if you want it to open like an app.

Does it read other games?

Yes: Pokémon and Dragon Ball cards too, all in the same collection.

Can I show my binder to friends?

Yes. Share a binder and anyone with the link sees the cards and their market prices, never what you paid or your email. Turn the link off any time.
