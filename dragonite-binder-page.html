The Dragonite binder page

Put one Dragonite from each era in the nine pockets, oldest top-left and newest bottom-right, and the page reads like a timeline. The Fossil Dragonite from 1999 opens the top row, and Mega Dragonite ex from Ascended Heroes, 2026, closes the bottom one, right after the 151 set's Dragonite: card 149, for Pokédex number 149.

One pocket that breaks the pattern

Pocket two is Dark Dragonite from Team Rocket. Every other Dragonite on the page is the friendly one; this one has been taken by Team Rocket and drawn with a meaner look. Next to the Fossil Dragonite, the top row starts with the same Pokémon twice, a year apart, in two moods.

Three layouts, compared

1. Oldest to newest. The page pictured above. One Dragonite per era, read like a timeline.

2. Dratini to Dragonite. Dragonite evolves from Dragonair, which evolves from Dratini. Put Dratini, Dragonair and Dragonite across each row, one row per set, so every row shows the whole evolution and the page shows three eras.

3. Kanto's seventeenth page. In a Pokédex binder in number order, nine Pokémon a page, Dragonite is number 149, so it lands in pocket five of page 17: the very middle of the page, with Dratini and Dragonair just before it and Mewtwo and Mew just after.

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type Dragonite. The builder lists Dragonite cards with the set each one comes from. Add a word from the set name, such as Dragonite Fossil, to see fewer cards.
  3. Tap one card for each pocket, oldest first. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Dragonite cards are there?

Dozens, once promos, theme decks and every version count. That is far too many for one page, so a Dragonite page is a choice of nine: one per era, one per card type or your nine favourite drawings.

Which Dragonite starts the page?

The holo Dragonite from Fossil, 1999, card 4 of 62: Dragonite's first card in English.

Which Dragonite ends the page?

Mega Dragonite ex from Ascended Heroes, 2026, the gold one, card 295 of 217. Right before it, in pocket eight, sits the Dragonite from the 151 set: card 149, and Dragonite is Pokédex number 149.

Where does Dragonite go in a Pokédex binder?

Dragonite is number 149. With nine pockets a page in number order, it lands in pocket five of page 17, the middle of the page, with Dratini and Dragonair right before it.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
