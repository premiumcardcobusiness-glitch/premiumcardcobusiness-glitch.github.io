Built for how Dragon Ball is printed

  • Two games, one scan. Fusion World and the Dragon Ball Super Card Game are separate games with separate prices; each card is matched to its own.
  • Alternate arts are priced apart. A Fusion World Secret Rare with one star or two is a different printing with a different price. When two printings look alike, the card is marked unsure, so you tap the right one.
  • Leaders and gold-leaf cards are read like any other card: name, set, number and market price.

What "free" means here

  • 1,000 card scans every 30 days, up to 300 a day. One scan is one card identified and priced.
  • Your first three weeks are bigger: 2,000 scans, 500 a day, and every Pro tool.
  • No payment details. When the three weeks end you simply stay on Free; nothing is charged.
  • No cap on your collection. Keep as many cards as you like. A free account holds one binder; Pro holds up to 20.

One card or a whole page

Add card photographs a single card. Page photographs a 9-pocket binder page and reads every pocket, so a binder of Dragon Ball cards goes nine at a time instead of one. Each card lands in your collection with its set, number and market price, and the page shows its total.

Where the prices come from

TCGplayer market prices, matched to the exact printing where we can and refreshed every morning. They are estimates for your own records, not offers to buy.

Yes: Pokémon and One Piece cards too, all in the same collection.

Yes. Share a binder and anyone with the link sees the cards and their market prices, never what you paid or your email. Turn the link off any time.
