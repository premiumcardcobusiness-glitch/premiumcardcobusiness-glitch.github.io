The Snorlax binder page

Put one Snorlax from each era in the nine pockets, oldest top-left and newest bottom-right, and the page reads like a timeline. The Jungle Snorlax from 1999 opens the top row, and the shiny Snorlax from Paldean Fates, 2024, closes the bottom one.

One pocket that breaks the pattern

Pocket two is Rocket's Snorlax ex. Every other card on the page is just Snorlax; this one belongs to Team Rocket, and its name says so. Put it next to the Jungle Snorlax and the top row tells a small story: the same sleepy Pokémon, then the same Pokémon working for the villains.

Three layouts, compared

1. Oldest to newest. The page pictured above. One Snorlax per era, read like a timeline.

2. Munchlax beside Snorlax. Snorlax evolves from Munchlax, a baby Pokémon that came much later than Snorlax itself. Put a Munchlax in the left pocket of each row and two Snorlaxes after it, one row per era, so every row shows the small one and the big one.

3. Kanto's sixteenth page. In a Pokédex binder in number order, nine Pokémon a page, Snorlax is number 143, so it lands in pocket eight of page 16: the middle of the bottom row, between Aerodactyl and Articuno.

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type Snorlax. The builder lists Snorlax cards with the set each one comes from. Add a word from the set name, such as Snorlax Jungle, to see fewer cards.
  3. Tap one card for each pocket, oldest first. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Snorlax cards are there?

Dozens, once promos, theme decks and every version count. That is far too many for one page, so a Snorlax page is a choice of nine: one per era, one per card type or your nine favourite drawings.

Which Snorlax starts the page?

The Snorlax from Jungle, 1999: Snorlax's first card in English. The holo is card 11 of 64; card 27 of 64 has the same art without the shine.

Which Snorlax ends the page?

The shiny Snorlax from Paldean Fates, 2024, card 202 of 91.

Where does Snorlax go in a Pokédex binder?

Snorlax is number 143. With nine pockets a page in number order, Snorlax lands in pocket eight of page 16, between Aerodactyl and Articuno.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
