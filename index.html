How it works

Three steps. No typing.

  1. Snap a page

    Hold your phone over a binder page. Nine cards, one photo.

  2. Check the reads

    Each card comes back named and priced. One it isn't sure of is marked, so it never looks sure.

  3. Keep it

    Your binder, page for page, in your pocket. Prices refresh every morning.

A show day

From the first buyer to the last box.

  1. Before the doors open

    Post your table

    One code for the whole show, one tap in the app.

  2. In the aisle

    Buyers find you

    Someone types “umbreon” and gets your table, the binder, the page and the pocket.

  3. A friend's card sells

    The split is done

    Your cut comes off and the app keeps a running total of what they're owed.

  4. After the show

    Pack up

    Your table comes off the show page by itself. With Pro, list what's left on eBay from the scan.

Questions

Before you start.

What does the free account include?

1,000 card scans a month (up to 300 a day), as many cards as you like, and your first three weeks with every Pro tool. No payment details. When the three weeks end, you stay on Free.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Beltstra runs in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Which cards does it read?

Pokémon, One Piece and Dragon Ball cards.

What if it reads a card wrong?

A card it isn't sure of is marked, so it never looks sure. Tap the right printing, or scan it again closer and flat.

Where do the prices come from?

TCGplayer market prices, matched to the exact printing where we can and refreshed every morning. They are estimates for your records, not offers to buy.
