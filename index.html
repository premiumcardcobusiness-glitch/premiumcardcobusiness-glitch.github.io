Piggy Bank

Every cash sale lands in your Piggy Bank.

Sell a card for cash and the money goes in by itself. Take money out for rent, gas or next show's table and see exactly where it went this month. It can't go below zero, and money that belongs to someone else is kept apart from what's yours.

Undo

A wrong tap is one tap to fix.

Picked the wrong copy or typed the wrong price at a busy table? Undo puts the card back, takes the cash back out of your Piggy Bank, and fixes your profit and your friend's page. If the cash is already spent, it tells you instead of breaking your numbers.

The scanner

Point your phone at a card. Get the exact printing and today's price.

Beltstra reads the card's name, set and number and prices it from TCGplayer's market data, updated daily. Cards that share a number can be worth very different amounts, so it checks the printing, and when it isn't sure, it tells you instead of guessing. Every card you scan is saved to your collection, with a one-tap eBay listing when you want one.
