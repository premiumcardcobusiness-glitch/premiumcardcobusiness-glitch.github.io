The Mew binder page

Put one Mew from each era in the nine pockets, oldest top-left and newest bottom-right, and the page reads like a timeline. The Southern Islands Mew from 2001 opens the top row, and the shiny Mew ex from Paldean Fates, 2024, closes the bottom one.

Two pockets that break the pattern

Eight pockets are a pink Mew on its own. Pocket six is a Tag Team card from Unified Minds, so Mew shares it with Mewtwo, the Pokémon made from its genes. Pocket nine is the shiny Mew from Paldean Fates, and shiny Mew is blue. Put it last and the page ends on its one blue card.

1. Oldest to newest. The page pictured above. One Mew per era, read like a timeline.

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type Mew. The builder lists Mew cards with the set each one comes from. Add a word from the set name, such as Mew Fates Collide, to see only that set's cards.
  3. Tap one card for each pocket, oldest first. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Mew cards are there?

Dozens, once promos, jumbo cards and every version count. That is far too many for one page, so a Mew page is a choice of nine: one per era, one per form or your nine favourite drawings.

Which Mew starts the page?

The Mew from Southern Islands, 2001, a holo card with Mew floating in a rainbow sky.

Why is one Mew blue?

Shiny Mew is blue instead of pink. The Mew ex from Paldean Fates in pocket nine shows the shiny Mew.

Where does Mew go in a Pokédex binder?

Mew is number 151, the last Pokémon of Kanto. With nine pockets a page, it lands in pocket seven of page 17, bottom-left, right after Mewtwo in pocket six.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
