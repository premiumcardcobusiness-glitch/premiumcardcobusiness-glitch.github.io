Plan details. Please read before you buy

All paid plans are auto-renewing subscriptions: monthly plans renew every month, annual plans renew every 12 months, at the prices shown above, until you cancel. You can cancel anytime from your account or by emailing us, and you keep access for the period you already paid for. Annual billing equals ten months' price for twelve months of access (two months free).

Scan allowances are rolling windows, not calendar months: Pro includes 10,000 scans per rolling 30 days with a burst limit of 1,000 per rolling 24 hours; Studio includes 6,000 per 30 days and 800 per 24 hours. One scan = one card identified and priced. Your live usage is always visible inside the app. Unused scans don't roll over.

The Free plan is 1,000 card scans per rolling 30 days (up to 300 per rolling 24 hours; 2,000 scans and 500 a day during your first 21 days). There is no limit, on any plan, on how many cards you keep in your collection or your binders. It requires no payment details and never converts into a paid plan by itself. Your own cards, one person you sell for, and every scanner tool work on it; your first 21 days include every Pro tool. If we ever change the Free plan for new members, accounts that already exist keep the plan they signed up with. Choose Pro any time to sell for more people and list on eBay from the app.

Taxes may apply and will be calculated at checkout. All prices in USD. Checkout is securely handled by Paddle, our authorized reseller and Merchant of Record.

What you need to run it

A phone or computer with a camera and a web browser. That's it: there is no app to download and no software to install. Card recognition runs on our servers; you sign in with your email and scan.
