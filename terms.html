1. The Service

Beltstra is a web app for people who collect, trade and sell trading cards. Today it does these things:

  • Scanning and pricing. You photograph a card or a binder page; the app identifies the cards and shows a market price for each. Prices are market estimates, not offers.
  • Your collection and binders. Cards you keep go into your collection, arranged in binders page by page, with the photos and notes you choose to keep on them.
  • Sharing. You can share a binder by link, list it on Explore, and post a show table so people at a card show can find you. What you share is visible to anyone with the link or on those pages; see section 3.
  • Selling for others. You can track cards you sell for other people (a consignor, a friend sharing your table), each person's share of a sale, and what you have paid them; each person gets their own no-login page.
  • The Piggy Bank. A record of the cash from your card sales and what you spent it on.
  • Buys between members. A vendor can price another member's binder pages and record a cash buy.
  • Slab Guard orders. Physical card protectors sold through our shop (section 7).

You use the Service in a web browser or installed on your phone's home screen. You sign in by email and password, or with your Google account (and with Apple where we offer it). Some features need a paid plan or the free trial; the Pricing page says which.
