Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Beltstra Co ("we", "us"), a sole proprietorship based in Aubrey, Texas, USA, collects and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

Payment information

We do not collect or store your card or bank details. All payments are processed by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com, who collects the information needed to complete your purchase (such as name, email, billing country, and payment details) under their own privacy policy.

License information

To operate your membership we store a license identifier associated with your purchase and the email address you used, so we can issue and verify your license. When you sign in or scan, we receive your license token to confirm it is active.

Card images and usage

When you scan a card, the image is sent to our servers to identify the card and is deleted after processing — we do not keep your card photos. We record per-scan usage (a count, timestamp, and basic device/connection information) to enforce your plan's scan allowance and to detect account sharing or automated abuse.

Links page visits

When you open our links page (the page our printed QR code opens) or tap one of its buttons, your browser sends a small visit notice to our server. We record the time, which button was tapped, your browser type, and your network address with its last part removed. We use this only to count visits. No cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device.

Your eBay account, if you connect it

If you connect your eBay account, we keep the sign-in token eBay gives us, so the Service can create the listings you ask for and show your open eBay orders on the Pack orders screen. We only read your orders; we never change them. While you pack, that screen shows each buyer's first name and last initial so you can match the order to its envelope. We do not store buyer names or addresses. When you mark an order packed, we keep its order number, the listing title and what eBay pays you for the card, so your collection and profit stay correct. You can remove the Service's access at any time in your eBay account settings.

Friends

You can add friends in the app by scanning each other's friend code in person. The code changes every minute and does not contain your email address. Your friends see the name you chose and, unless you choose to keep your cards private, the cards in your collection: card names, printings, how many you have and their market prices. Friends never see what you paid, your cash, cards you sell for other people, or your email address. You can remove a friend at any time.

2. How we use information

  • To issue, verify, and support your membership license
  • To respond to support requests you send us
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations (largely handled by Paddle)

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers necessary to run the Service — primarily Paddle.com for payment processing. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

We keep license and contact information for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing us. For payment data held by Paddle, contact Paddle directly.
