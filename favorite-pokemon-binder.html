How to make a binder for your favorite Pokémon

Pick what counts, put the cards in release order with the oldest first, and leave the last page partly empty. Oldest first means every new card goes on the end, so you never have to move the rest of the binder. Then count the cards you plan to collect and divide by nine to know how many pages to buy.

Decide what counts

This is the choice that sets the size of the binder. Pick one before you buy pages.

RuleWhat goes in
One of each cardEvery different card of your Pokémon, one copy each. The usual place to start.
Every versionReverse holos, promos and other languages as well. The long project, and the biggest binder.
Art cards onlyFull arts, illustration rares and special art cards. A short binder that is all showpieces.
The whole lineEvery stage, such as Gastly, Haunter and Gengar, side by side on each page.

You can change your mind later. Starting small and adding a rule is easier than starting with every version and giving up halfway.

Put them in an order that never needs reshuffling

  • Oldest first. Read the page left to right, top to bottom, from the first card ever printed to the newest. A new set only ever adds cards at the end.
  • One era per page. Older cards on one page, newer eras on the next. Leave the spare pockets on each page empty so a missing card has a home waiting for it.
  • A centerpiece in the middle. On a showpiece page, put your best card in pocket five, the center of a nine-pocket page, and build the other eight around it.

Nine printings of the same Pokémon look very different side by side: different artists, different eras, different frames. That is the fun of a one-Pokémon binder. Each page shows how the same favorite has been drawn over the years.

How many pages you need

Count the cards you plan to collect, divide by nine and round up. Then think about one more page for the cards that have not been printed yet. Forty cards fill four pages and part of a fifth, and the five free pockets on page five are where the next new cards go. The binder page calculator does the sum for you.

Plan the first page before you sleeve

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Tap your Pokémon if it is in the row of favorites at the top, or type its name. The builder lists its cards with the set each one comes from.
  3. Tap cards in the order you want them. Pockets fill in the order you tap, so tap your oldest card first.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How do I start a binder for one Pokémon?

Decide what counts (one of each card, every version, or art cards only), put the cards in release order with the oldest first, and leave the last page partly empty so new cards go on the end.

How many cards does one Pokémon have?

It depends on the Pokémon and on what you count. Popular ones such as Pikachu and Charizard run into the hundreds once every version counts. Count the cards you plan to collect, then divide by nine for the pages.

Do TAG TEAM and cameo cards count?

That is your rule to set. A simple split: cards that name your Pokémon go in the main run; cards where it only appears in the artwork go on a cameo page at the back.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
