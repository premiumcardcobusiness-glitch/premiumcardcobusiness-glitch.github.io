Why collectors look for another app

In collector groups the same question comes up again and again: which app scans my cards without a subscription? Many scanner apps limit free scanning and ask for a monthly or yearly plan to keep going. If that is where you are, here is exactly what Beltstra gives you for free, so you can compare it with whatever you use now.

What you get on the free plan

  • 1,000 card scans a month, up to 300 a day (2,000 and 500 a day in your first three weeks).
  • Nine cards in one photo: point your phone at a binder page and every pocket is read.
  • Every card priced from TCGplayer market prices, matched to the exact printing where we can, refreshed every morning.
  • Keep as many cards as you like. There is no cap on your collection or your binders.
  • Binders that match your real one, page by page and pocket by pocket, with share links for friends.
  • Runs in your phone's browser. iPhone, Android or a computer. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
  • The free plan never turns into a paid one by itself, and it needs no payment details.

How a binder page scan works

  1. Open Beltstra and tap Scan, then Page.
  2. Lay the binder flat and take one photo of the whole page.
  3. Each of the nine pockets comes back with the card's name, set and market price, plus the page total. A card the app is not sure of is marked, so it never looks sure.

A full 360-card binder is about 40 photos instead of 360.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan is 1,000 card scans per rolling 30 days with no payment details. Paid plans exist for people who sell cards for others and list on eBay; you never need one to scan and price your own collection. See pricing.

Which cards does it read?

Pokémon, One Piece and Dragon Ball cards.

Do I have to install anything?

No. Beltstra runs in the browser on your phone. There is nothing to download from an app store.

What happens to my scan photos?

They are deleted from our server after the read. A page you share never shows what you paid or your email. Privacy Policy.

Can I get my collection out again?

Yes. Download your collection as a spreadsheet (CSV) any time from My Collection.
