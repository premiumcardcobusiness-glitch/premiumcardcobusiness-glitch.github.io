What it does that a price checker doesn't

Whose card was that?

Put a card in under someone's name when it comes in. When it sells, the app splits the money on the cut you agreed, keeps a running total of what each person is owed, and records the payout when you hand it over. No spreadsheet, no arguing at the next show.

The Piggy Bank

Cash in, cash out, what a card cost you and what it made. You can see the day's take and what's actually yours out of it, because what you owe is already taken off.

Pack check, before the envelope

Photograph the card you're about to ship. It reads it against the eBay order it's going into and says green, amber or red — right card, look again, or stop. Wrong-card returns cost more than the card.

List it on eBay in one tap

Any card the app has photographed can go up as an auction or Buy It Now without you typing a title, picking a category, or taking another photo. Your own eBay account, your own shipping policy.

A binder that knows where the card is

Your binder, page for page, pocket for pocket. Someone asks for the Umbreon and the app says "Chase binder, page 4, pocket 6" instead of you flipping through nineteen pages at the table. One photo of a page fills the whole page at once. Turn on "Show it to anyone" and the same binder is one link, one QR code on the table, and one search box on the show's page.
