The Gardevoir binder page

Put one Gardevoir from each era in the nine pockets, oldest top-left and newest bottom-right, and the page reads like a timeline. The EX Ruby & Sapphire Gardevoir from 2003 opens the top row, and a gold Mega Gardevoir ex from the Mega Evolution set, 2025, closes the bottom one.

One pocket that breaks the pattern

Pocket six is a Tag Team card: Gardevoir shares it with Sylveon. It is the only card on the page with two Pokémon on it, so it sits in the middle of the middle row, where the eye lands first.

Three layouts, compared

1. Oldest to newest. The page pictured above. One Gardevoir per era, read like a timeline.

2. Ralts to Gardevoir. Gardevoir evolves from Kirlia, which evolves from Ralts. Put Ralts, Kirlia and Gardevoir across each row, one row per set, so every row shows the whole evolution and the page shows three eras.

3. Hoenn's fourth page. In a Pokédex binder that starts each region on a fresh page, Hoenn's 135 Pokémon fill exactly 15 pages. Gardevoir is number 282, so it lands in pocket four of page four, right after Ralts and Kirlia, and its whole family sits together on one page.

Try it before you sleeve

Moving nine cards around a real page is slow, and every move risks a scuff. Try the order on screen first, as many times as you like.

  1. Open the free page builder. No account needed.
  2. Type Gardevoir. The builder lists Gardevoir cards with the set each one comes from. Add a word from the set name, such as Gardevoir Sandstorm, to see fewer cards.
  3. Tap one card for each pocket, oldest first. Pockets fill in the order you tap.
  4. Tap See page. Tap a card to take it out, then add another, until the page looks right.
  5. Keep the page with a free account, then sleeve the real cards in the same order.

Questions

How many Gardevoir cards are there?

Dozens, once promos, theme decks and every version count. That is far too many for one page, so a Gardevoir page is a choice of nine: one per era, one per card type or your nine favourite drawings.

Which Gardevoir starts the page?

The holo Gardevoir from EX Ruby & Sapphire, 2003, card 7 of 109: Gardevoir's first set in English.

Which Gardevoir ends the page?

Mega Gardevoir ex from the Mega Evolution set, 2025, here in its gold print, card 187 of 132.

Where does Gardevoir go in a Pokédex binder?

Gardevoir is number 282. If Hoenn starts on a fresh page, its 135 Pokémon fill exactly 15 pages, and Gardevoir lands in pocket four of page four, right after Ralts and Kirlia.

Is the page builder free?

Yes. Planning a page needs no account. Keeping it makes a free account, which never asks for payment details.
