One card or a whole page

Add card photographs a single card. Page photographs a 9-pocket binder page and reads every pocket, so a stack of binders goes nine cards at a time instead of one. Each card lands in your collection with its set, number and market price, and the page shows its total.

Tips for a clean read

  • Lay the card or binder page flat and fill the frame with it.
  • Use even light and tilt away from lamp glare on holo cards.
  • If a card is marked unsure, tap the right printing or scan it again closer and flat.

Where the prices come from

TCGplayer market prices, matched to the exact printing where we can and refreshed every morning. They are estimates for your own records, not offers to buy.

It reads Pokémon, One Piece and Dragon Ball cards.

Can I show my binder to friends?
